# Leimert Phone Company / Sankofa Red
with Ben Caldwell/[KAOS Network](https://www.kaosnetworkz.com/) and Wesley Groves, 2024 - present

Sankofa Red is one of the phones developed through [Leimert Phone Company](https://leimertphonecompany.net/), a project I have been working on with Ben Caldwell and KAOS Network. Leimert Phone Company repurposes old payphones to share neighborhood stories and preserve local culture in Leimert Park. The project began in 2013 through the work of Ben Caldwell, KAOS Network, and a broader team of collaborators. Sankofa Red was its first physical prototype, first publicly shown in November 2013. A more detailed history is available on the [project website](https://leimertphonecompany.net/prototypes/sankofa-red/).

Ben Caldwell, Wesley Groves, and I updated the phone for the November 2026 grand reopening celebration of the Vision Theatre. The historic theater is next door to KAOS Network and had been closed for more than 15 years. For this deployment, Sankofa Red featured selections from Kaya Dantzler’s [We Love Leimert archive](https://weloveleimert.la/#/memory), which visitors could explore using the phone’s keypad.

Sankofa Red has a long history of public deployments and exhibitions. I began working on the phone for [*No Prior Art: Illustrations of Invention*](https://www.lapl.org/no-prior-art) at the Los Angeles Public Library (September 14, 2024 – May 11, 2025). I reprogrammed the phone and helped produce a recording session for its updated content, recorded by Justin Asher at KAOS Network. This version was also exhibited in [*KAOS THEORY: The Afrokosmic Media Arts of Ben Caldwell*](https://artandpractice.org/exhibitions/exhibition/kaos-theory-the-afrokosmic-media-arts-of-ben-caldwell/) at Art + Practice, presented with the California African American Museum (October 12, 2024 – March 8, 2025).

Our goal is to make recordings from each phase of that history available through the keypad. The expanded archive would span the original 2013 deployment, *No Prior Art*, the Vision Theatre celebration, and future additions.

The phone runs on a Raspberry Pi and is based on software originally written by Damon Turner. For the November 2026 deployment I prepared the We Love Leimert recordings and updated the Python script to support them. Following that deployment I continued developing the [phone software](https://github.com/caseyanderson/leimertPhoneCompany): I created a local prototype that simulates the handset and keypad and then developed a [multi-project version](https://github.com/caseyanderson/leimertPhoneCompany/tree/prototyping) that allows listeners to move between different archives. We are also discussing a version that records new contributions from community members directly through the phone.
