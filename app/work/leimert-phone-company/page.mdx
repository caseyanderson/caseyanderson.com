# Leimert Phone Company / Sankofa Red
with Ben Caldwell/[KAOS Network](https://www.kaosnetworkz.com/) and Wesley Groves, 2024 - present

Sankofa Red is a repurposed payphone that shares neighborhood stories and preserves local culture in Leimert Park. The inaugural physical prototype of [Leimert Phone Company](https://leimertphonecompany.net/), it debuted publicly in November 2013. The project grew out of the work of Ben Caldwell, KAOS Network, and a broader team of collaborators; a more detailed history is available on the [project website](https://leimertphonecompany.net/prototypes/sankofa-red/).

Ben Caldwell, Wesley Groves, and I updated the phone for the November 2026 grand reopening celebration of the Vision Theatre. The historic theater is next door to KAOS Network and had been closed for more than 15 years. This version featured selections from Kaya Dantzler’s [We Love Leimert archive](https://weloveleimert.la/#/memory), which visitors could explore using the keypad.

I began working on the phone for [*No Prior Art: Illustrations of Invention*](https://www.lapl.org/no-prior-art) at the Los Angeles Public Library (September 14, 2024 – May 11, 2025). I reprogrammed the phone and helped produce a recording session for its updated content, recorded by Justin Asher at KAOS Network. This version was also exhibited in [*KAOS THEORY: The Afrokosmic Media Arts of Ben Caldwell*](https://artandpractice.org/exhibitions/exhibition/kaos-theory-the-afrokosmic-media-arts-of-ben-caldwell/) at Art + Practice, presented with the California African American Museum (October 12, 2024 – March 8, 2025).

The phone runs on a Raspberry Pi and is based on [software](https://github.com/caseyanderson/leimertPhoneCompany) originally written by Damon Turner. For the Vision Theatre deployment I prepared the We Love Leimert recordings and updated the Python script to play them. I then created a local prototype that simulates the handset and keypad and developed a [multi-project version of the phone software](https://github.com/caseyanderson/leimertPhoneCompany/tree/prototyping) that allows listeners to move between different archives.

Our goal is to make recordings from each phase of the phone’s history available through the keypad, spanning its original 2013 deployment, SKIN stories, PROXIMITY, No Prior Art, the Vision Theatre celebration, and future additions. We are also discussing a version that records new contributions from community members directly through the phone.
