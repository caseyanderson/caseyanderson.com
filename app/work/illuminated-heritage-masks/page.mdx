# Illuminated Heritage Masks
with Ben Caldwell/[KAOS Network](https://www.kaosnetworkz.com/), Dara Caldwell-Ross, and Wesley Groves, April 5, 2025

<Cover
  src="illuminated-heritage-masks/dsc05303.jpg"
  caption="Illuminated Heritage Masks at Octavia Lab · Photograph by Elizabeth Flores"
/>

I designed and led [Illuminated Heritage Masks](https://www.lapl.org/events/illuminated-heritage-masks-kaos-network), a hands-on workshop at the Los Angeles Public Library’s [Octavia Lab](https://www.lapl.org/octavia-lab), as part of my collaboration with Ben Caldwell and KAOS Network. With help from Dara Caldwell-Ross and Wesley Groves, participants created illuminated masks using simple circuits, conductive materials, and LEDs.

The workshop grew out of the hands-on technology activities [Elizabeth Chin](https://elizabethjchin.com/) and I developed with Ben Caldwell and KAOS Network in Leimert Park. Ben proposed bringing digital technology into the mask-making tradition of the LA Commons / Leimert Park Village Festival of Masks. Illuminated Heritage Masks brought that idea to Octavia Lab as part of the library’s [*No Prior Art*](https://www.lapl.org/no-prior-art) program series.

Todd Lerew, curator of *No Prior Art*, helped connect our ongoing work at KAOS Network with the library and supported bringing the workshop to Octavia Lab.
